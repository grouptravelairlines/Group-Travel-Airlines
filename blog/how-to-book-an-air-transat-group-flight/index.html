How To Book An Air Transat Group Flight?

Group Travel Airlines

An expedition with lots of members seems a muddled and tedious task. But it is easily manageable because several airlines cater to such travel expectations, just like Air Transat. With this airline, passengers can seamlessly make their shared voyage joyful with its special group travel service. Whether it is a family gathering, sports events, a business meeting, or any other occasion, by choosing its services, flyers make their air journey more exciting and memorable. However, to get the Air Transat group flights, they have to follow a different booking process from the regular one. Along with this, they avail of ample privileges and group deals. Therefore, the details and information regarding the same are all provided herein.

What Are the Group Booking Terms and Conditions of Air Transat?

Traveling with large groups has become quite simple on Air Transat through its specialized services. However, to access these features, one has to adhere to the group booking conditions of the airline. By meeting the minimum requirements, one would be able to request an Air Transat group quote easily without any hassle. Not only that, but they can also avail themselves of the exclusive perks that come along with the service and make heedful decisions too. For more information about the group booking conditions of Air Transat, check the pointers below.

  1. The group feature will become accessible if there are ten or more members.
  2. Passengers are also required to meet the deadlines on time; otherwise, it would hamper their bookings.
  3. All members must be flying to the common destination under the same flight segment.
  4. It is recommended to carry valid travel documents to the airport.

How to Book Group Travel on Air Transat?

Making reservations for large parties is quite convenient on Air Transat. Here, the reservations are managed under a centralized system that ensures better coordination among the group members. Besides this, to make Air Transat group bookings, individuals are allowed to use its email address to contact the help desk representative, which is groupquotes@transat.com. This is the easiest way to request bookings and allows flyers to directly inquire about doubts and other issues too. To learn more about the subsequent steps, read ahead.

  1. Connect with the airline by using the above-mentioned email ID.
  2. Then, communicate the travel requirements and other necessities with them.
  3. Soon, the airline will share an official quote based on the given information.
  4. Accept it before the validity period expires and make the payment as instructed in the offer.
  5. Once done, submit the names of the flyers and receive confirmed travel documents in the registered email account.

Does Air Transat Offer Group Rates?

Yes, Air Transat provides group rates. Moreover, these special deals could be available on both domestic and international routes operated by the airline. But when numerous flyers are included in one reservation, then acquiring Air Transat group deals becomes possible. Further, these fares can be limited to groups and may also include exclusive facilities that are not commonly available on regular bookings.
